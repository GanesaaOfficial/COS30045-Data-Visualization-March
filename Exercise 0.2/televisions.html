Television Energy Consumption

Explore the latest information about TV energy efficiency and power consumption in Australia.

Screen Technologies

Different television technologies have varying energy consumption profiles. Understanding these differences can help you make informed purchasing decisions.

LCD (LED)

Market Share: Most common (>70%)

Power Consumption: Moderate

Characteristics: LED-backlit LCD panels offer good brightness and affordability. They dominate the market due to competitive pricing and reliable performance.

Average Power: 150-250W for 55" models

OLED

Market Share: Premium segment (~5-10%)

Power Consumption: Low to Moderate

Characteristics: Organic LED technology provides superior picture quality with perfect blacks and superior contrast. Each pixel emits its own light.

Average Power: 100-180W for 55" models

LCD (Standard)

Market Share: Declining (<5%)

Power Consumption: Higher

Characteristics: Traditional LCD without LED backlighting. Mostly found in budget small-screen TVs under 35".

Average Power: 50-100W for 32" models

Screen Size vs Energy Consumption

There is a clear relationship between screen size and power consumption. Larger screens generally require more power to operate.

Size Range Analysis

  • 24"-32": Small screens, often used as computer monitors or bedroom TVs. Power consumption: 50-100W
  • 43"-50": Mid-sized screens, popular for living rooms. Power consumption: 100-160W
  • 55"-65": Large screens, most popular size range for households. Power consumption: 150-250W
  • 75"-86": Extra-large screens for commercial or large entertainment spaces. Power consumption: 250-350W

Energy Efficiency Tips

💡 Choose Energy Stars

Look for televisions with high Energy Star ratings. Higher ratings indicate better energy efficiency.

📏 Right Size Matters

Select a screen size appropriate for your viewing distance. A 65" TV in a small room uses unnecessary power.

⚙️ Adjust Settings

Reduce brightness and enable power-saving modes. Most TVs use 10-20% less power in eco mode.

🔌 Unplug Idle Devices

TVs in standby mode still consume power. Turn off and unplug when not in use for extended periods.

Market Overview

The Australian television market is dominated by several major brands offering a wide range of models across different price points and technologies. Leading brands include Samsung, LG, Hisense, TCL, and Sony, each with their own range of energy-efficient options.
