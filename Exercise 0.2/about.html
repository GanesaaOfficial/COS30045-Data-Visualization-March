About Energy Australia

Dedicated to providing transparent, data-driven insights about appliance energy consumption in Australia.

Our Mission

Energy Australia is committed to helping consumers, researchers, and policymakers understand and optimize energy consumption in household appliances. We believe that informed choices lead to better environmental outcomes and cost savings for Australian households.

Our mission is to make energy consumption data accessible, understandable, and actionable for everyone.

What We Do

🔬 Research & Analysis

We analyze comprehensive datasets on appliance energy consumption to identify trends, patterns, and opportunities for efficiency improvements across the Australian market.

📊 Data Visualization

We transform complex energy data into clear, interactive visualizations that help people understand the relationship between appliance specifications and power consumption.

📚 Education

We provide educational content about energy efficiency, helping consumers make informed purchasing decisions that benefit both their wallets and the environment.

🌍 Sustainability

We support Australia's environmental goals by promoting awareness of energy consumption and encouraging adoption of more efficient appliances.

About This Project

Exercise 0.2: Energy Website is part of the COS30045 Data Visualisation course at Swinburne University. This website demonstrates fundamental web development skills including:

  • HTML: Semantic markup and multi-page structure
  • CSS: Professional styling with responsive design
  • JavaScript: Navigation and interactive features
  • Data: Real-world appliance energy consumption metrics
  • GitHub: Version control and collaborative development

Data Sources

The energy consumption data presented on this website is based on:

  • Australian market television models and specifications
  • Manufacturer power consumption ratings
  • Energy rating certifications from regulatory bodies
  • Industry standard measurements and methodologies
