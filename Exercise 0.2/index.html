Understanding Energy Consumption

Energy consumption by household appliances is a crucial factor in both environmental sustainability and household budgets. In Australia, the average household spends a significant portion of their electricity bill on major appliances.

📺 Televisions

Modern televisions have become more energy-efficient over the years. Learn about screen technologies and power ratings on our Televisions page.

♻️ Sustainability

Understanding energy consumption helps us make better choices for the environment. Every kilowatt saved makes a difference.

💰 Cost Savings

Choosing energy-efficient appliances can significantly reduce your monthly electricity bills and long-term costs.

Quick Facts

  • Televisions account for approximately 3-5% of household electricity consumption
  • Modern LED/LCD TVs are 20-30% more efficient than older plasma models
  • OLED technology offers superior picture quality with competitive power consumption
  • Screen size significantly impacts power consumption - larger screens use more energy
  • Energy Star certified appliances help reduce your carbon footprint
