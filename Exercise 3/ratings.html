Question 6

Energy Star Ratings vs Screen Size

Australia's Energy Rating label lets consumers compare efficiency at a glance. But do star ratings actually reflect screen size, or is the story more complex?

Chart 6 — Star Rating vs Screen Size (Grouped Box Plot)

Higher star ratings strongly correlate with smaller screen sizes. The 6-star models are almost exclusively compact screens under 35". As screen size grows, achieving a high star rating becomes harder because absolute power draw rises faster than panel efficiency improvements can compensate. A 3.5-star 85" TV may actually be more efficient for its size than a 4-star 55" TV — the star rating reflects absolute consumption, not efficiency per inch.

A grouped box plot (one box per star rating) is used because star ratings are discrete categories. A scatter plot would result in vertical columns of overlapping dots — the box plot clearly shows how the distribution of screen sizes shifts across efficiency tiers.
