Question 3

Which Brands Offer the Most Models?

Australia's TV market is competitive and diverse. This chart shows which brands have the broadest product range — from global giants to budget newcomers.

Chart 3 — Top Brands by Number of Models

Samsung and LG lead the market by model count, covering every size tier from compact budget TVs to premium 86" flagship panels. Hisense and TCL have rapidly expanded their Australian ranges with competitive mid-range pricing. Sony and Panasonic focus on fewer but higher-quality models, targeting the premium segment. Budget brands like Kogan compete on price with a wide range of entry-level screens.

A horizontal bar chart sorted in descending order is used here. When there are many brand labels, a horizontal layout keeps all names legible — a vertical chart would cause text overlap on the x-axis.
