Screen Technology & Size Distribution

What types of screen technology are sold in Australia — and which sizes are most common? These two charts explore the shape of the market.

Chart 1 — Screen Technology Distribution

LCD (LED) dominates the Australian TV market, making up the vast majority of models across all size ranges. OLED panels remain a smaller premium segment offering deeper blacks and higher contrast. Traditional LCD (without LED backlight) appears mostly in small, budget-oriented screens under 35".

A vertical bar chart is used here because the human eye judges bar length more accurately than pie-slice angles — making this a clearer choice than a pie chart for comparing category frequencies.

Chart 2 — Screen Size Distribution

Screen sizes range from 24" to 86". The market clusters most strongly around 43"–65" — the living-room sweet spot. Very large screens (75"+) are a growing segment, while screens under 32" are shrinking as small TVs are replaced by tablets and monitors.

A histogram is used because screen size is a continuous variable — binning into ranges (e.g. 40"–49") shows the distribution correctly. A standard bar chart would be inappropriate here.
